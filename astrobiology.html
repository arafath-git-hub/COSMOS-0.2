Context Matters

Sky observation and human belief.

For much of history, observing celestial movements was closely connected with calendars, religion, navigation, and interpretations of earthly events. Over time, astronomy and astrology developed into different disciplines.

Historical Importance Astrological systems influenced literature, royal courts, calendars, and cultural traditions.
Symbolic Interpretations Zodiac signs and planetary placements carry meanings within particular traditions.
Scientific Evidence Astrology's claims about predicting personality or life events have not been reliably demonstrated by scientific testing.
