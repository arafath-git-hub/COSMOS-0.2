Research Field 02

Astrobiology

Investigate life's origins, the conditions that allow organisms to survive, and the possibility of life beyond Earth.

What Is Astrobiology?

Astrobiology is an interdisciplinary field that studies the origin, evolution, distribution, and future of life in the universe. It combines biology, chemistry, geology, planetary science, and astronomy to investigate whether life could exist beyond Earth.

Earth is currently the only world known to support life. By studying organisms in extreme environments and examining other planetary environments, scientists can better understand what life may require.

Major Research Areas

Origins of Life

Explore how simple chemical substances may have developed into increasingly complex systems capable of supporting life.

Habitability

Investigate liquid water, chemical ingredients, energy sources, and environmental stability on planets and moons.

Biosignatures

Search for possible signs of biological activity while checking whether non-biological processes could explain the same evidence.

Extreme Life on Earth

Study microorganisms that survive in hot springs, acidic waters, deep rocks, deserts, and other extreme environments.

Ocean Worlds

Examine icy moons that may contain liquid oceans beneath their surfaces and investigate their potential habitability.

Life Beyond the Solar System

Study exoplanets and their atmospheres to understand whether distant worlds may have conditions compatible with life.

Where Do Scientists Search?

  • Mars: Ancient environments may have been suitable for microbial life, making preserved rocks important research targets.
  • Europa: Jupiter's icy moon is believed to have a subsurface ocean beneath its frozen crust.
  • Enceladus: Saturn's moon releases plumes of material from an underground ocean into space.
  • Exoplanets: Scientists study distant planetary atmospheres for clues about their environments.
  • Earth: Microorganisms and ancient rocks help researchers understand the limits and history of life.

How Is the Research Conducted?

Robotic Exploration

Rovers, orbiters, and spacecraft examine environments and collect measurements that cannot easily be obtained from Earth.

Laboratory Experiments

Researchers test how organic molecules behave under different temperatures, pressures, radiation levels, and chemical conditions.

Telescope Observations

Astronomers analyze the light passing through or emitted by exoplanet atmospheres to investigate their composition.

Important Scientific Questions

  • How did life first emerge on Earth?
  • Could microbial life have existed on ancient Mars?
  • Could subsurface oceans on icy moons support living organisms?
  • What evidence would be convincing proof of life beyond Earth?
  • How can scientists distinguish a real biosignature from a false positive?
