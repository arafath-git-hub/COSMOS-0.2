About COSMOS

COSMOS is a space exploration website designed to make astronomy and planetary science easier to discover. Explore planets, learn about historic missions, and investigate the questions that continue to inspire scientists.

Start exploring → Our purpose
Our purpose

Making space easier to explore

The universe can feel enormous and complicated. COSMOS organizes space-related topics into clear, accessible sections so visitors can explore one idea at a time—from the planets in our Solar System to distant galaxies and unanswered questions about the cosmos.

The project combines educational explanations, mission histories, scientific concepts, and links to trusted sources. It is intended as a learning resource and a starting point for further exploration, not a replacement for original scientific publications or mission data.

What guides the project

Our principles

Good science communication should help people become curious, ask better questions, and understand what evidence can—and cannot—tell us.

Scientific accuracy

Present established findings carefully, distinguish hypotheses from confirmed observations, and link to reputable scientific resources.

Accessible learning

Explain complex topics in clear language without losing the important scientific meaning.

Curiosity first

Encourage visitors to ask questions, compare evidence, and continue learning beyond a single page.

Continuous improvement

Science changes as new observations arrive. Educational content should be reviewed and updated when reliable new information becomes available.

A note on science

Explore. Question. Discover.

Space science is an ongoing process. Some facts are well established, while other ideas remain under investigation. COSMOS encourages readers to look at the evidence, check original sources, and stay open to new discoveries.

Unless a page explicitly states otherwise, COSMOS is an independent educational project and is not an official NASA or ESA website. Mission names and agency resources belong to their respective organizations.
