Our mission

Making the universe easier to explore.

The universe is vast, complex, and full of unanswered questions. COSMOS organizes space-related subjects into accessible sections so visitors can explore one idea at a time—from the planets in our Solar System to distant galaxies and the physics of extreme environments.

The project brings together educational explanations, mission histories, scientific concepts, and links to trusted resources. It is designed as a starting point for learning and further investigation, not a replacement for original scientific publications or mission data.

The foundation

Built around curiosity and evidence.

Good science communication helps people ask better questions, evaluate evidence, and understand both what we know and what remains uncertain.

Scientific accuracy

Present established findings carefully, distinguish hypotheses from confirmed observations, and point readers toward reputable scientific resources.

Accessible learning

Explain complex subjects in clear language without losing their important scientific meaning or context.

Curiosity first

Encourage visitors to ask questions, compare evidence, and keep learning beyond a single page.

Continuous improvement

New observations can change our understanding. Educational content should evolve as reliable scientific knowledge advances.

A commitment to science

Explore. Question. Discover.

Space science is an ongoing process. Some findings are well established, while other ideas remain under investigation. COSMOS encourages readers to examine evidence, consult original sources, and stay open to new discoveries.

COSMOS is an independent educational project and is not an official NASA or ESA website. Agency names, mission names, and linked resources belong to their respective organizations.
