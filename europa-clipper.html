Europa Clipper

NASA's Europa Clipper mission is designed to investigate Europa, one of Jupiter's largest moons. Beneath its icy surface, Europa is believed to contain a global ocean of liquid water. The mission will examine whether this moon has environments that could support life.

Space Agency NASA
Launch Date October 14, 2024
Destination Europa, Jupiter's moon
Primary Goal Assess habitability

What Is Europa Clipper?

Europa Clipper is a robotic spacecraft built to make repeated close flybys of Europa. Rather than orbiting Europa directly, the spacecraft will orbit Jupiter and pass by the moon many times, collecting measurements from different locations.

Its large solar arrays generate electricity far from the Sun, while its scientific instruments investigate Europa's ice shell, surface, possible ocean, and surrounding environment.

Why Is Europa Interesting?

Europa's surface is made largely of water ice. Several lines of evidence suggest that a salty liquid-water ocean exists beneath this frozen exterior. Tidal forces from Jupiter and interactions with neighbouring moons may provide energy that affects Europa's interior.

On Earth, life exists in environments where sunlight is limited or absent. Scientists therefore investigate whether Europa could have liquid water, useful chemical ingredients, and energy sources that might make it habitable.

A potentially habitable environment is not proof of life. Europa Clipper is designed to assess habitability, not to directly detect living organisms.

Mission Objectives

  • Measure the thickness and properties of Europa's icy shell.
  • Investigate the ocean's potential depth, salinity, and other properties.
  • Map Europa's surface geology and identify regions of scientific interest.
  • Analyze the moon's surface composition and search for organic and other chemical materials.
  • Study Europa's atmosphere and its interaction with Jupiter's radiation environment.

Scientific Instruments

  • Imaging system: Maps surface features in detail.
  • Ice-penetrating radar: Investigates the ice shell and structures beneath the surface.
  • Spectrometers: Study the chemical composition of surface materials and gases.
  • Magnetometer: Measures magnetic fields that can help scientists investigate the subsurface ocean.
  • Plasma and dust instruments: Examine particles and the space environment around Europa.

Journey to Jupiter

After launching in October 2024, Europa Clipper began its journey toward the Jupiter system. The spacecraft is planned to arrive in 2030. It will then conduct a series of close Europa flybys to gather data from multiple regions of the moon.

Mission schedules can change. Check NASA's official mission page for the latest trajectory, arrival plans, and operational updates.

Why Does This Mission Matter?

Europa Clipper will help scientists understand whether an ocean world beyond Earth has the chemical and physical conditions that could support life. Its findings will also improve our understanding of icy moons and ocean worlds throughout the Solar System.
