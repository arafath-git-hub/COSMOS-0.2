Research Fields

Explore the science of worlds

Planetary science brings together astronomy, geology, physics, chemistry, and atmospheric science to understand the worlds around us.

FIELD / 01

Planetary Geology

Study planetary surfaces, impact craters, volcanoes, mountains, and the geological processes that shape other worlds.

FIELD / 02

Planetary Atmospheres

Investigate clouds, winds, atmospheric chemistry, weather systems, and climate across different planets.

FIELD / 03

Moons and Natural Satellites

Explore icy moons, subsurface oceans, tidal heating, and the complex relationships between moons and their planets.

FIELD / 04

Exoplanet Science

Study planets orbiting other stars and investigate their sizes, orbits, atmospheres, and possible environments.

FIELD / 05

Planet Formation

Examine how dust, gas, and small rocky bodies develop into planets within disks around young stars.

FIELD / 06

Planetary Habitability

Investigate water, energy sources, chemical ingredients, and environmental conditions relevant to life beyond Earth.

The Big Picture

Every world tells a story.

A planet's surface, atmosphere, magnetic field, and moons preserve clues about how it formed and changed over time. By comparing worlds, scientists can better understand Earth and the processes that shape planetary systems throughout the galaxy.

Comparative Planetology Compare worlds to discover the physical processes they share and the ways they differ.
Robotic Exploration Use orbiters, landers, rovers, and spacecraft to investigate distant environments.
Remote Observation Analyze light, spectra, radar measurements, and images to study worlds from afar.
Worlds Beyond Our Solar System Use observations of exoplanets to expand our understanding of planetary diversity.
